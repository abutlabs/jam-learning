Learning Lasair

Graypaper 0.8.0

How a JAM client is built, with lasair, an OCaml client that passes every published 0.8.0 conformance vector, as the executable reference: the protocol, the codec and Merklization, the state transitions, the PVM, block import, conformance and networking, plus M1 Understanding, chapter by chapter of the Graypaper with self-tests. Most lessons show lasair's code; if you are building a JAM Prize implementation, read the note on the Prize rules first.

The abutlabs JAM stack

lasair: an independent OCaml JAM client. It passed M1 (block import) on Graypaper 0.7.2, passes every published 0.8.0 vector, and is building M2: authoring, JAMNP-S networking and GRANDPA finality. Its source is not public yet; the course quotes the code it discusses. Its course.

jamswap: an order-book exchange that runs as a JAM service, and the harness that runs it on nets of any mix of clients. Its collaboration guide is how a client team or a service joins. Build JAM together.

observability: metrics, logs and dashboards for JAM networks with any mix of clients (MIT). Its course.

How it is checked. Everything here is held to what can be verified: lasair against the official conformance vectors and fuzzer, the labs by running them, the protocol against the Graypaper text. Where something is wrong, open an issue.
