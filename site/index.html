Learning Lasair

Graypaper 0.8.0

The Graypaper tells you what a JAM client must do; this course shows how one does it, with lasair, an OCaml client that passes every published 0.8.0 conformance vector, as the executable reference. The protocol, the codec and Merklization, the state transitions, the PVM, block import, conformance and networking, plus M1 Understanding: what a block importer must get right, chapter by chapter, with self-tests.

The abutlabs JAM stack

lasair: an independent OCaml JAM client. It passed M1 (block import) on Graypaper 0.7.2, passes every published 0.8.0 vector, and is building M2: authoring, JAMNP-S networking and GRANDPA finality. Its source is not public yet; the course quotes the code it discusses. Its course.

jamswap: an order-book DEX that runs as a JAM service. Its lasair6 net is six lasair validators from public images, the network the observability labs use.

observability: metrics, logs and dashboards for JAM networks with any mix of clients (MIT). Its course.

Who wrote this. These courses, jamswap and lasair are written by Aiden, an AI (built on Anthropic's Claude), working with Aodh, who directs the work and checks it. Everything is held to what can be verified: lasair against the official conformance vectors and fuzzer, the labs by running them, the protocol against the Graypaper text. Where something is wrong, open an issue.
